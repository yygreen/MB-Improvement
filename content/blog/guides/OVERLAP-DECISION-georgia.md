# Settling the Georgia overlap (2026-08-12)

**Decision: new page at `/post/aba-therapy-funding-georgia`. Both existing
Georgia posts survive. Trim their coverage sections and link them to the
guide.**

This is the New Jersey answer, not the North Carolina one, and the test
that separated them is the same one both times: **is the existing page
already the map?**

## The two candidates

`/post/aba-therapy-services-in-georgia-overview` (5,818 words) is the
closer of the two. It mentions Ava's Law and $35,000, and it carries an
"ABA Therapy Coverage in Georgia" section with insurance details,
pre-authorization and Medicaid. But its spine is provider and setting:
certification, education and training, in-home versus other settings,
what the landscape looks like. It answers "what is ABA in Georgia and who
delivers it", not "who pays and what do I do when they say no".

`/post/how-georgia-laws-support-autism-services` (5,138 words) is further
away still. Its sections are support networks, disability services,
housing programmes and family support. It mentions Ava but not the cap,
not the age limit, not the appeal route. It is a resources page wearing a
legal title.

Neither opens by laying out the systems and telling you which one applies
to you. That is what made North Carolina different: that page's own first
paragraph was already the map, so writing a second one would have been
building our own competitor.

## Why a new URL rather than absorbing the overview

Absorbing would mean taking a services-and-settings page and replacing it
with a funding guide, which throws away the intent it currently serves and
keeps a slug that does not match the funding query. The North Carolina
slug matched its query almost word for word; `aba-therapy-services-in-
georgia-overview` does not.

So Georgia follows New Jersey: a purpose-built guide at a clean slug, with
the older pages kept for the questions they actually answer.

## Required follow-up, not yet done

The overlap is settled in principle but not yet in the CMS. Two edits are
outstanding, and until they are made the coverage sections compete:

1. **`/post/aba-therapy-services-in-georgia-overview`** — cut "Insurance
   Coverage Details" and "Limitations and Pre-Authorization" down to a
   short paragraph that links to the guide. Its Medicaid subsection can
   stay brief and also link. Also fix "Clinic-Based Therapy", which
   implies a clinic we do not run.
2. **`/post/how-georgia-laws-support-autism-services`** — its "Georgia's
   Autism Insurance Laws" and "Mandatory Coverage Reporting" sections
   should point at the guide rather than restate the law. Also check
   "Top Autism Centers" for competitor names and for the same clinic
   implication.

Both are pre-existing copy-gate problems, flagged rather than silently
fixed, because trimming 11,000 words of legacy copy is a separate piece of
work from shipping the guide.

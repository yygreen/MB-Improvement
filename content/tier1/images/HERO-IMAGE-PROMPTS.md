# Hero image prompts, 12 state service pages

Replaces the grey `Hero image placeholder` div in the right column of each Tier 1 hero.
Artifact version with copy buttons: see the session link.

## Spec

    aspect        4/3, set by .mm-hero__media; anything else is cropped by object-fit:cover
    delivery      WebP, >= 1600x1200 (existing hub heroes are 1066x800)
    composition   subject slightly right of centre; the image sits beside the headline
    safe area     outer 8% expendable (20px radius + responsive crop)

## Style block, prepended to all 12

Candid documentary lifestyle photograph. Real, lived-in American family home, not a set.
Natural window light only. Warm neutral palette. 35mm, eye level, f/2.0, shallow depth of
field. Unposed and mid-moment: nobody looks at the camera. Photographic realism, no
illustration, no HDR.

Derived from the hero already live on /in-home-aba-therapy: woman and small boy at a
kitchen table with wooden blocks, backlit window, plants on the sill, child's drawing on
the fridge.

## Exclusions, appended to all 12

Exclude: clinic, therapy office, classroom, waiting room. No medical equipment, clipboards,
charts, scrubs, lanyards or ID badges. No logos, signage, screens or any legible text. No
puzzle-piece imagery or autism-awareness symbolism. No posed stock smiles, no eye contact
with the camera, no thumbs-up. No watermarks.

THREE OF THESE ARE NOT STYLISTIC:

  - No clinic. Programme-wide copy gate, and these pages sell in-home specifically. An
    institutional-looking room contradicts the page it sits on.
  - No puzzle piece. Widely rejected by autistic people and much of the advocacy world.
    On a BCBA-owned provider it would do real damage.
  - No badges or scrubs. The practitioner reads as a person in ordinary clothes.

## Localization approach

The three hub pages already use PLACE photography: Carolina Theatre for NC, a Savannah
townhouse for GA. These twelve deliberately do not. They stay inside the home, because
that is what the pages are about, and carry the state in the architecture and what is
visible through the window.

    New Jersey       colonials and capes, hardwood, plaster, wide painted trim, cast-iron
                     radiator under a double-hung window, close neighbouring rooflines,
                     mature maples, chain-link or picket fence, cool four-season light
    Georgia          brick ranch and craftsman bungalow, ceiling fans, deep eaves, screened
                     porch off the kitchen, loblolly pine, crepe myrtle, red clay, bright
                     humid light
    North Carolina   Piedmont brick ranch and craftsman, front porch with rockers past the
                     door, quilts, family photographs, dogwood and longleaf pine, soft light

Casting is named per frame so the twelve do not come back as one demographic repeated.
Ages step up by service, toddler through older teenager, matching the page copy.

## Two decisions before commissioning

Stock or generated. These brief a photographer or a stock search almost unchanged. Generated
is faster and cheaper but carries real risk on a healthcare site: hands, faces, and text
artefacts. If generating, budget a careful pass on hands and any surface that might render
pseudo-text.

Consistency. Twelve images that each look good but do not look related will read worse than
twelve plainer ones that do. Produce all twelve in one session against the same style block
and review them as a contact sheet rather than one at a time.

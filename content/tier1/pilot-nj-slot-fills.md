### Coverage that matters for transition planning

| Coverage element | New Jersey detail |
| --- | --- |
| State mandate | P.L. 2009, c. 115 ("Health Benefits Coverage for Autism and Other Developmental Disabilities") — signed August 2009, effective February 9, 2010 |
| Age limit | Statute references under 21; federal MHPAEA parity generally makes age caps unenforceable on plans subject to parity |
| NJ FamilyCare (Medicaid) | Covers ABA for children under 21 via EPSDT, effective April 2020. Income up to 355% FPL for children under 19. We're enrolled with the major NJ FamilyCare MCOs. |
| Other pathways | NJ Children's System of Care, NJ Early Intervention System (NJEIS) for children under 3 |

Lead-in sentence (new copy, not clinical — style only):
> Coverage does not stop because your teen is getting older. The rows below are the parts
> of New Jersey's coverage picture that matter most in the transition years. The full
> state-by-state detail lives on our [New Jersey ABA therapy page](/aba-therapy-in-new-jersey).

Why these four rows: the age-limit row and the FamilyCare under-21 row answer the question
every transition-age family has ("does coverage run out?"); the mandate row is the legal
basis both rest on; the pathways row hands off to Children's System of Care, which
Section 2 of the draft already discusses (PerformCare → DDD handoff), so the table and
the body reinforce each other instead of duplicating the hub.
